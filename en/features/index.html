Features

LayKagu helps you plan where your furniture goes before you move or rearrange, in 3D and on a 2D floor plan.

Create your floor plan

  • From an image: From a photo of a flyer, a floor plan image from a listing site, a PDF, or a document scan, LayKagu reads room names, sizes, and dimensions along with walls, doors, and windows to make a draft. You review and fix it before it becomes your floor plan
  • From a listing page URL: Reads candidate floor plan images and details such as the layout, area, and orientation (for sites it can't read, it shows how to save and import the image instead)
  • From a template: Start from 1R to 3LDK shapes and adjust the sizes
  • By drawing: Draw walls and rooms, and add doors, windows, kitchens, columns, and other fixtures. You can also trace over an image
  • Supports multiple floors, north direction, and ceiling height

Gather your furniture

  • Catalog: Choose from 124 furniture shapes and enter the width, depth, and height; the shape fits. Legs and handles keep their natural proportions
  • From a product page URL: Reads the name, size, price, shop, and color, and suggests a similar shape
  • From a photo: Suggests a similar shape from the furniture type and color in your photo
  • Stock: Keep a list of furniture you own, plan to buy, or are considering, with a budget total
  • Send product and listing pages to LayKagu from the Share menu in Safari and other apps

Place and check

  • Long-press to pick up furniture and place it in 3D or 2D, with wall snapping and alignment
  • View in 3D from above, straight down, or at eye level as you walk around. The minimap shows where you are
  • Layout check: Shows overlaps, collisions with walls and columns, door swings, furniture blocking windows, narrow walkways, and the space needed to use each piece, with symbols and text
  • Delivery route: Checks whether large furniture fits through the doors from the entrance to the room (including tilting it, furniture that can be taken apart, elevators, and shared hallways)
  • Fit to window: Fits curtains and similar items to the width and height of a window

Compare

  • Create several layouts and compare them side by side in 2D or with linked 3D views, with marks for the differences and a table of walkway widths and issue counts

Export and share

  • Export the plan and furniture list to PDF (A4 or A3, with 1:50 or 1:100 chosen automatically), PNG, or CSV
  • Save a project as a file (.laykagu) and open it on a family member's device
  • Use the same projects on your iPhone and iPad with iCloud sync

Designed for everyone

  • With VoiceOver alone, you can create a floor plan, place furniture, check, and export
  • Supports larger text, Increase Contrast, and Reduce Motion
  • On iPhone Duo, your editing state is kept when you fold or unfold, and you can use 3D and 2D side by side when unfolded

Sizes and checks are estimates. Measure the real items and spaces before you buy or deliver furniture.

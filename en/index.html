Build your room from a floor plan image or a listing page, then try the furniture you own or plan to buy. Check walkways, door swings, and the delivery route before you move.

Create your floor plan

Read a floor plan image from a rental flyer or a listing site and trace it into rooms. You can also start from a template or draw it yourself.

Gather your furniture

Pick from 124 catalog items and enter the size. You can also add furniture from a product page URL or a photo.

Place and check

See overlaps, door swings, and narrow walkways, marked with symbols and text. Check whether large furniture fits through from the entrance.

Compare and export

Compare layouts side by side, and export the plan and furniture list to PDF, images, or CSV.

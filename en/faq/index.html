Guide & FAQ

Getting started

  1. After the introduction on first launch, open the sample project (2LDK) to try things out
  2. Tap New Project to create a project, then Add Floor Plan. Choose a floor plan image, a listing page URL, a template, or drawing
  3. Add furniture to the Stock at the bottom of the editor. The basic way is to pick from the catalog and enter the size
  4. Long-press furniture in the Stock and drag it onto the 3D view or the 2D plan
  5. Tap the check mark at the top to see a list of overlaps and narrow walkways. Add layouts to compare, or export to PDF

Frequently asked questions

Are the sizes and checks accurate?

They are estimates. Floor plans read from images and sizes imported from external pages may differ from the real ones. Before buying or delivering furniture, measure the room and the furniture. You can fix floor plan sizes after they are read.

Which websites can LayKagu read?

LayKagu isn't limited to particular sites. It looks for product names, sizes, prices, and floor plan images written on the page. It doesn't read pages that require sign-in, pages blocked by bot protection, or pages from websites whose terms prohibit automated access. In those cases, save the floor plan image and import it, or pick from the catalog and enter the size.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Everything except reading URLs and iCloud sync works without a connection. Images are also read on your device (even with Private Cloud Compute turned on for reading rooms, plans are read on your device only while you're offline).

Are floor plan images sent anywhere?

Only on supported devices and only if you agree. In that case, one image is sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute to read the number, names, sizes, and rough positions of the rooms (never to our servers). It works on devices that support Apple Intelligence while you're online. You can turn it off at any time in Settings under "Floor Plan Reading." Privacy Policy

Can I use the same project on iPhone and iPad?

Turn on iCloud Sync in Settings to sync between devices signed in to the same Apple Account (the change takes effect the next time you open the app).

I want to show a project to my family

Use Export to File in the project menu to create a .laykagu file and send it with AirDrop or Messages. When opened in LayKagu on their device, it is imported as a new project. You can also send just the plan as a PDF or image.

What does the free version include?

One project, up to two layouts per floor plan, and about 40 catalog items. The sample project doesn't count. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks all features.

Can I use it on Mac or Apple Vision Pro?

LayKagu is made for iPhone and iPad. It is not offered on Mac or Apple Vision Pro.

How do I delete my data?

Use Move to Trash in a project's menu (projects in the Trash are permanently deleted after 30 days). Deleting the app also deletes the data on your device. If you used iCloud sync, you can delete the iCloud data in the iPhone Settings app under your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

If this doesn't help, please contact Support.

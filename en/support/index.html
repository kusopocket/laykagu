Support

If you need help, please check the Guide & FAQ first.

Contact

Email: laykagu.dev@gmail.com

Including the following helps us look into it sooner:

  • Your device model (for example, iPhone 17 or iPad Air) and iOS or iPadOS version
  • The LayKagu version (in the app, Settings > Version)
  • What happened and the steps you took
  • A screenshot, if possible

Replies may take a few days. Please send floor plan images or project files only if we ask for them.

Purchases and refunds

In-app purchases are made through the App Store. On another device or a new device, use Settings > Unlock Full Version > Restore Purchases in the app. To request a refund, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

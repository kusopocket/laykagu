Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Kusopocket (the "Provider") handles information in LayKagu, an app for iOS and iPadOS (the "App"). This English version is a translation for reference. If it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Provider

  • Name: Kusopocket
  • Contact: laykagu.dev@gmail.com

2. Information the Provider collects

The Provider does not collect your personal information through the App. The App has no account registration or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

The floor plans, furniture, layouts, photos, images, and other data you create in the App are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud sync, in your iCloud. The Provider never sees or receives this data.

3. Access to external websites

When you enter the URL of a listing page or a product page to read it, the App connects directly from your device to that website and downloads the page and its images. Information that comes with the connection, such as your IP address and device type, is sent to that website and handled under that website's own policies.

The App fetches a page only once, when you start reading it. It does not fetch pages repeatedly or fetch pages that require sign-in. It does not fetch pages from websites whose terms prohibit automated access.

4. Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only to scan documents such as floor plan flyers
  • Only the photos you choose are used, as a floor plan tracing or a furniture photo
  • Images are read on your device (text and shape recognition, furniture type and color suggestions). The only exception is reading the rooms of a floor plan with your consent, described in Section 6

5. Apple Intelligence (on-device processing)

On supported devices, the App uses Apple's on-device language model (Foundation Models) to read information from pages and images. This processing happens on your device and nothing is sent to the Provider or any third party.

6. Reading floor plan rooms (Apple's Private Cloud Compute)

On supported devices, only if you agree, the App sends one floor plan image to Apple's Private Cloud Compute (Apple Intelligence models running on Apple's servers) to read the number, names, sizes (tatami), and rough positions of the rooms.

  • The App asks for your consent on screen before it sends an image for the first time. You can turn this off at any time in Settings under "Floor Plan Reading"; when it is off, plans are read on your device only
  • The image is sent only to Apple's Private Cloud Compute. It is never sent to the Provider's servers or any other third party, and the Provider never receives the image or the result
  • Text recognition and wall detection stay on your device; only the image is sent
  • Apple states that request data sent to Private Cloud Compute is not retained after the response. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to Apple's handling
  • Each Apple Account has a daily usage limit. When the limit is reached or you are offline, plans are read on your device only

7. iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, the App's data is synced with your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). The data is stored in your iCloud and the Provider cannot see it. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to iCloud.

8. Crash reports

Only if you have agreed to share analytics with Apple in your device settings, the Provider may receive crash reports for the App through Apple. They are used only to fix problems and never to identify you.

9. Purchases

Purchases of the App and in-app purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment information; the Provider does not receive it.

10. Sharing with third parties

Because the Provider does not collect your information, it does not share it with any third party.

11. Children

The App can be used by people of any age. The Provider does not collect children's information either. Minors should get permission from a parent or guardian before making in-app purchases.

12. Changes

The Provider may change this Policy when necessary. Important changes will be announced on the official website or in the App. The changed Policy takes effect when it is posted on the official website.

  • Effective date: November 9, 2026

13. Contact

For questions about this Policy, please contact laykagu.dev@gmail.com.
